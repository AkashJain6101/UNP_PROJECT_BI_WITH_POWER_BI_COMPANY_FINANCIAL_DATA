# UNP_PROJECT_BI_WITH_POWER_BI_COMPANY_FINANCIAL_DATA


As part of our Value-Added Course with UNP Education, we conducted a financial data analysis using Power BI to gain valuable business insights. Our analysis focused on sales performance, profit trends, regional profitability, discount impact, and product performance. By utilizing Power BI’s data visualization and analytical tools, we identified key trends and provided strategic recommendations to enhance business efficiency.

Key Findings & Insights:

1.Recorded a 249.46% increase in profit, driven by higher sales, pricing adjustments, and demand fluctuations.

2.Uncovered regional differences in profit margins, emphasizing the need for localized strategies to improve profitability.

3.Evaluated the role of discounts, discovering that while they boosted sales, excessive discounting on specific products led to lower profit margins.

4.Assessed product profitability, revealing that some low-margin products, despite strong sales, negatively impacted overall profits, while high-margin products played a key role in driving business growth.


Strategic Recommendations:

1.Refine pricing models by adopting dynamic and value-based pricing strategies.

2.Implement a well-structured discounting approach to optimize sales without compromising profit margins.

3.Enhance inventory control through demand forecasting, reducing instances of overstocking and stock shortages.

4.Strengthen sales initiatives in high-growth regions to capitalize on existing market potential.

This project highlighted the importance of data-driven decision-making through Power BI, providing actionable insights for long-term financial and operational improvements.
